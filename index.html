Interactive plot with district loss on the horizontal axis and statewide loss on the vertical axis. Each iso-misrepresentation contour joins loss pairs with the same total M at the selected weight. Moving toward the lower left cannot increase M. Pareto-efficient top-seat allocations form the frontier; an optimum lies on the lowest attained contour, and muted dominated points are no better on either loss and strictly worse on at least one. Changing the statewide weight redraws the contours and may change the optimal seat total.
