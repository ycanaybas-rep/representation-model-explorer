What this page does—and does not—measure

The page does not optimize all U.S. districts as one national election. It applies the selected weight within each available state election and sums those independently chosen delegations. States and seats absent from the supplied panel remain unassigned.

The source contains normalized Democratic and Republican district vote shares, not raw vote totals. The displayed D:R ratio is therefore the equal-district mean used by the model, not a turnout-weighted national popular vote. See Data construction for coverage, normalization, and proxy rules.
